# a11y

Accessibility is not a pass at the end; these are cheap during and expensive after.

1. **Semantic elements.** A `<button>` is a button. A div with a click handler
   has no keyboard, no role, no focus, no announcement.
2. **Keyboard, whole flow.** Tab through it. Can you reach everything, in a
   sane order, and see where you are? Can you escape a dialog? Is focus trapped
   where it should be and returned when it closes?
3. **Visible focus.** Never remove the outline without replacing it with
   something better.
4. **Contrast for real.** 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI
   boundaries. Compute it over what is actually behind the text — a translucent
   panel over a photo is not its token value.
5. **Names.** Every control, every image. An icon-only button needs a label.
6. **Do not rely on colour alone** to carry meaning.
7. **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`** and `prefers-color-scheme`.
8. **Errors** announced, tied to their field, and describing the fix.

Test with the keyboard only, and at 200% zoom. Both find real bugs in minutes.
