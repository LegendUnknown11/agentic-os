# literature

1. **Write the question down** as a sentence you could answer yes or no to.
2. **Search for the measurement, not the opinion.** Papers, benchmarks, docs
   from the people who built the thing. Blog posts about blog posts are noise.
3. **Read the numbers and the method.** What was measured, on what, against
   what baseline. A percentage with no baseline is decoration.
4. **Check it applies to you.** Different scale, different workload, different
   hardware — most published wins do not transfer unchanged.
5. **Take the mechanism, not the conclusion.** "Recency pruning of tool results
   cut tokens 64%" is only useful once you know which of your costs is analogous.
6. **Then measure it yourself** on your own workload — see `measure-first`.

Cite what you used. An unsourced claim is worth nothing to whoever reads you next.
